Where Hubble is looking from, right now.

Just under five hundred kilometres up, a telescope the size of a bus has been circling us since 1990. It sees no weather and no sunset. This page follows it, quietly.

How far up is up?

Space is not far away — it is just straight up. On this scale each step is ten times the one before.

Everything is on the same line, and each step is ten times the one before: the Station sits closer to you than many capitals are to each other, the Moon a thousand times further, Neptune ten thousand times further again.
