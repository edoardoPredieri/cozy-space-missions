The whole system at true scale, and the Earth magnified in a circle of its own — because at this scale everything on this site fits inside that one blue dot. Inside the circle, distances run on a ten-times scale so that four hundred kilometres and a million can share a frame; the directions are real. A filled mark goes round the Earth, a hollow one is far out at L2, and the line points at the Sun — which is why the hollow two sit opposite it.
