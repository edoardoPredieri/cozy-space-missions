        How to read the map

        The trail

        The amber line is the ground track: where the Station has been over the past hour, and where it will be over the next one. Each lap takes about ninety minutes.

        The shadow

        The darker band is the half of the Earth where it is night. The ISS is visible to the naked eye only while it is still in sunlight and the ground below is already dark.

        The circle

        The thin circle is the horizon: from anywhere inside it, with a clear enough sky, the Station is above the horizon line.
